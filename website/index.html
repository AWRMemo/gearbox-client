The architecture has three layers

Capture
Enrichment
Structure

Capture. Text, quotes, links, snippets — whatever you notice and choose to keep. Paste directly, use the browser extension, or share from any app on mobile.

Enrichment. On-device AI reads your captures, extracts meaning, generates summaries, adds tags. Everything processes locally. Your signal never travels to a server.

Structure. Your captures form connections. Topics emerge. You see the patterns in your attention — because the map belongs to you, not a cloud provider.

We cannot see your data.

We cannot give anyone access. End-to-end encryption with keys derived from your password. We hold no keys. The core client is open-source. Verify for yourself.

The desktop client, the mobile apps, and the core engine are all Apache 2.0 on GitHub. No hidden components. No encrypted blobs only we control. Typefaces load from Google Fonts (no cookies, no tracking).

Your data stays in your machine. Your sync server. Your encryption keys. Your decisions about who to follow and what to share. This is not privacy by policy. This is privacy by architecture.
