## 1. Why We Exist

### 1.1 The Problem

In 2026, knowledge work is defined by abundance without retention. The average professional consumes thousands of words of generated text daily, captures hundreds of fragments, and retrieves almost none. The tools that claim to "help you think" are optimized for production — faster output, more content, now. None are optimized for **remembering what mattered**.

The result is context collapse: a life of reading without connection, of saving without structure, of having accumulated thousands of signals and possessing no map.

### 1.2 The Shift

On-device intelligence changes the contract. For the first time, a personal tool can understand your context without exposing it. Not "privacy-respecting." Not "encrypted in transit." **Architecturally impossible to access.** The model runs inside your machine. Your highlights never leave. Your patterns never train someone else's product.

This creates a new category: tools that are **intimate by architecture** — not because of a policy, but because the math makes any alternative impossible.

### 1.3 Purpose

To give every mind a private, permanent place where attention becomes structure — so that no insight is lost, no connection is forgotten, and no thought is ever surveilled.

### 1.4 Vision

Become the default memory layer for digital life. Not a filing cabinet. Not a note-taking app. The silent, on-device engine that turns what you notice into what you know — and lets you share that knowing with others on your own terms.

### 1.5 Mission

Build tools so personal they feel like extensions of thought. Local-first. AI-native. Unapologetically private.

## 2. The Psychology

### 2.1 Intimacy by Architecture

Privacy is not a setting. It is a feeling. The tool that processes your context inside your own machine feels different from the tool that sends it to a server — even if both claim to be "secure." This warmth is not marketing. It is the emotional signature of local-first architecture.

### 2.2 Curation as Scarce Resource

AI has solved production. What remains scarce is human discernment — the act of selecting what matters from infinite noise. Every capture is a decision: *this was worth keeping.* Relay makes those decisions valuable by preserving them, connecting them, and making them publishable.

### 2.3 Agency Over Abundance

The product does not generate for you. It organizes what you have already chosen. You are the curator. The AI is the index. This distinction is the core value proposition: Relay amplifies human judgment, it does not replace it.

### 2.4 Memory as Identity

What you capture and revisit becomes who you are. The tool shapes the self. Every highlight is a vote: *this mattered to me.* Relay preserves those votes permanently, privately, and makes them navigable.

### 2.5 The Stream

A Stream is an auto-updating publication of your captures on a specific topic. It is not a blog post, a newsletter, or a social feed. It is a living curation — your perspective on a frequency, evolving as you encounter new signal. Subscribers do not follow you. They subscribe to your output on a specific wavelength.

## 3. Archetypes & Personality

### 3.1 The Herald (Portal / Gearbox.ai)

The website is not the product. It is the invitation. Its role is to announce, to beckon, to show the path. The Herald does not map territory — it points to where mapping happens.

**Voice:** Clear, anticipatory, confident. "This is what happens inside. Enter."

### 3.2 The Cartographer (Product / Gearbox Relay)

The app is the destination. Its role is to map, connect, and navigate. The Cartographer finds patterns in chaos, draws lines between points, and makes the unknown traversable.

**Voice:** Direct, precise, illuminating. "This connected to that. You captured this three times. Here is your map."

### 3.3 Resulting Personality

Warm, precise, unobtrusive. Speaks with the confidence of something that works — not the bravado of something that sells. Has the patience of a librarian and the taste of an editor. Never hyperbolic. Never cute. Direct, but never cold.

## 4. Tone of Voice

### 4.1 Principles

- **Direct:** No "revolutionary" or "game-changing." Say what it does. Your data stays here. Period.
- **Warm:** "Your context," "your mind," "your signal." Intimate, not corporate.
- **Precise:** Technical language used correctly, not performatively.
- **Fierce on privacy:** "We cannot see your stuff. We cannot give anyone access. It is architecturally impossible."
- **Humble on AI:** The AI is a tool, not a personality. It does not "think for you." It organizes what you have already thought.

### 4.2 Microcopy

- **First capture:** "Captured. Indexed. Yours."
- **Privacy reassurance:** "On-device by default. No cloud inference. Opt-in telemetry only. Your signal is yours."
- **Stream publish:** "You're about to relay this context set. Others can subscribe to your signal. Your private library stays sealed."
- **Paywall (4th Stream):** "You've built three transmissions. Unlimited Streams are available with Pro."
- **Creator fee:** "Your Stream earns. You decide what's fair. Minimum 10% covers costs. Suggested 15% fuels the open-source core."

## 5. Visual Identity

### 5.1 Philosophy

**Clarity from complexity.** Every element either carries meaning or gets out of the way. The system is built on one principle: signal against noise. No decorative gradients. No generic tech aesthetics. No stock photography.

### 5.2 Color

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Deep Space** | `#0A0A0F` | Primary background. The void — raw input, chaos. |
| **Signal Amber** | `#FFB000` | Human attention. Used sparingly — never as a fill, only as light. |
| **Enrichment Violet** | `#6B4EE6` | AI transformation. Distinct and specific. Used only where data is actively being enriched. |
| **Structure White** | `#E8E6E3` | Clarity, the output. Warm off-white, never pure white. |

### 5.3 Typography

- **Headings & Display:** Sharp serif with editorial weight (Cinzel or equivalent). Conveys permanence and craft.
- **Body & Data:** Humanist monospace (IBM Plex Mono or equivalent). Technical but approachable.
- **UI & Microcopy:** Clean sans-serif (Inter or equivalent). Neutral, fast, readable at small sizes.

**Rule:** No all-caps display type. No compressed tracking. Let the letterforms breathe.

### 5.4 Imagery

- **Product screens:** Always dark UI with amber and violet highlights. Never white backgrounds.
- **Abstract textures:** Light finding form in darkness — lens flares, caustics, particle systems, constellation maps. Topographies of signal.
- **Hands and artifacts:** A hand resting near a keyboard. A notebook beside a screen. Physical evidence of a thinking person. No faces.
- **Conceptual pairings:** Chaos beside its resolved form — a scattered point cloud next to its connected graph. The message: structure emerges.

### 5.5 Iconography

Custom icons where every icon is a diagram of the action. Consistent 2px stroke, rounded terminals, amber or steel on dark.

### 5.6 Layout

- **Asymmetrical grids with breathing room.** Like a well-organized workspace.
  - Never center all sections on the same vertical axis for more than two consecutive blocks.
  - Offset sections 10–20% left or right of center (e.g., Hero at +10vw, Problem at +8vw, Answer at −10vw, Trust at +14vw). Collapse to shared margins below 900px.
- **Depth through layering:** Subtle inner shadows, not glassmorphism. Panels feel grounded, not floated.
- **Flow diagrams:** Schematics showing how text moves through enrichment, search, and publication. The visual language of process.
- **Progressive enhancement:** All motion must degrade to a fully readable static state in browsers without scroll-driven animation APIs. No blank or broken elements.

## 6. Motion & Sound

### 6.1 Motion Language

- **Easing:** Custom cubic-bezier mimicking physical inertia. No linear fades. Elements settle like weighted objects.
- **Transitions:** The central motif is emergence — chaos aligning into structure. Quick, precise, grounded.
- **Loading:** A simple pulse or flow indicator. Never a spinner.
- **Data flow:** Amber particles moving through visible channels — attention becoming structure.

### 6.2 Sound (Optional)

All sound disabled by default. Single toggle: "Enable Workshop Sounds."

- **Capture:** Soft click of a precision mechanism engaging.
- **Enrichment complete:** Low, resonant tone — a signal locking into phase.
- **Stream relay:** Rapid, staccato clicks like a telegraph signal.
- **Error:** Dry, crisp disengage — a connection dropping cleanly.

## 7. The Web Experience

### 7.1 Core Concept

Gearbox.ai is the **portal**, not the destination. It does not replicate the product. It invites the visitor to install it.

The experience is a **quiet, typographic single-page experience** — designed for reading, not watching. The Herald archetype does not perform. It announces. It declares. Words emerge from stillness. The only active element is the visitor's cursor — a single point of amber light illuminating the text as it passes.

### 7.2 The Experience

1. **Landing:** A clear, immediate statement: "Your personal intelligence engine. Zero cloud. Zero compromise."
2. **The Declaration:** A single declarative phrase that states what this is.
3. **The Problem:** Context without statistics. Felt truth: "You save more than you can find."
4. **The Answer:** How it works. Emotional + architectural. "On-device. Local-first. Architecturally privacy."
5. **The Trust:** Open-source verification. "We cannot see your data. We cannot give anyone access."
6. **The Invitation:** Download. Platform badges. "Your personal transmission. Runs entirely on your machine."

### 7.3 Technical Constraints

- **Pure HTML + CSS + JavaScript.** No WebGL, no WebGPU, no canvas, no SVG canvas.
- **Single HTML file.** No build step. No bundler. View-source accessibility.
- **No cookies. No analytics. No external scripts beyond fonts.** Privacy-preserving by structure.
- **No interactive demo.** No sculpture. No particles. No generative systems. Text is the visual element.
- **Progressive enhancement for motion:** All scroll-driven or viewport-driven animation must degrade to a fully readable static state in browsers without `animation-timeline`, `IntersectionObserver`, or `requestAnimationFrame`. No blank elements, no broken layouts, no required polyfills. Use feature detection, not browser sniffing.

### 7.4 Featured Streams

The website may showcase **curated, read-only examples** of published Streams — descriptions, creator attribution. These are static HTML cards, not live web viewers. The message is: "This is what exists inside. Install to experience it."

### 7.5 Narrative Flow

1. **Curiosity** — the declaration, the promise
2. **Understanding** — the problem and the answer
3. **Trust** — the explicit architectural privacy
4. **Desire** — wanting this power daily
5. **Belonging** — installing the app, entering the private space

### 7.6 Visual Structure

| Section | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Fixed | Logo + CTA only. No navigation needed — the page is short. |
| Hero | Declaration | Core promise in minimal words. |
| Problem | Context | Why this exists. Felt truth, not data. |
| Answer | Explanation | How it works. Emotional + architectural. |
| Trust | Proof | Open-source verification. Privacy as architecture. |
| Call | Action | Download buttons. Platform badges. Beta email. |

### 7.7 Typography

- **Headings:** Cinzel (serif) — Conveys permanence and editorial weight.
- **Body:** IBM Plex Mono (monospace) — Technical but approachable.
- **Line-height:** 1.8 — Generous spacing for deep reading.
- **Max-width:** 52ch — Optimal line length for reading comprehension.

### 7.8 Cursor Glow

The only interactive element on the page:

- A small amber circle (~24–32px) follows the cursor at 8px offset, lagged by a lerp factor of ~0.16 per frame for physical weight.
- Has `filter: blur(8–10px)` for soft illumination, base `opacity: 0.1–0.4`
- **Proximity detection:** Glow must measure distance to all interactive elements (links, buttons, cards, inputs) within a radius of 80–120px. Opacity scales from base to peak (0.7–0.9) as the cursor enters this radius. Nearby text may receive a subtle amber text-shadow (0 0 18px rgba(255,176,0,0.12)) to reinforce the “illuminating the text as it passes” metaphor.
- Fades to `opacity: 0.1` after 3 seconds of inactivity
- **Disabled on touch devices entirely.** The glow is a mouse metaphor; on touchscreens it should not render to avoid half-functional artifacts.
- Disappears entirely when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`
- No JavaScript animation loops — smooth `requestAnimationFrame` only

### 7.9 Accessibility

- All content is semantic HTML (`<section>`, `<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<p>`)
- No canvas obscuring text
- No foreignObject rendering issues
- Full keyboard navigability with amber focus outlines
- Reduced-motion users see pure static typography
- Selection styled in amber for consistent visual language

### 7.10 Meta-Assets & Favicon

The Herald is an invitation — its presence in browser tabs, bookmarks, and social shares must carry the brand identity without external dependencies:

- **Favicon:** A single amber dot (`#FFB000`) on transparent dark background, 32×32 and 180×180 for touch. No gradients, no text.
- **Open Graph image (`og:image`):** A static 1200×630 PNG — deep space background, amber signal line, and the declaration “Your signal. Your structure. Your machine.” in Cinzel. No animation, no data URIs in the HTML.
- **Social share text:** Must include the declaration sentence or the hero sentence, never generic marketing copy.

## 8. Brand & Pricing Alignment

### 8.1 Pricing

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Unlimited captures, basic enrichment, 3 Streams, 1 device, 50 MB sync |
| **Pro Monthly** | $8/mo | Unlimited Streams, advanced enrichment, Themed Reviews, multi-device sync, 5 GB |
| **Pro Annual** | $80/yr | All Pro features, ~17% discount |
| **Founding Curator** | $60/yr | Grandfathered for life, capped at 5,000 subscribers. First 3 months only |

### 8.2 Creator Economics

**Beta Holiday:** 0% platform fee for 12 months from public launch. Creators keep 100%.

**Post-Beta: Contribution Model**

| Level | Fee | Purpose |
|-------|-----|---------|
| **Minimum** | 10% | Covers direct processing costs |
| **Suggested** | 15% | Fuels team and open-source development |
| **Generous** | 20%+ | Invests in the platform's future |

**Transparency:** A public contribution page shows aggregate costs and average contributions.

### 8.3 Pricing Psychology

- Whole-dollar prices only ($8, $80, $60). No .99 trickery.
- No per-seat enterprise tiers. Simple, personal, transparent.
- Free tier is genuinely generous. Upgrading feels natural, not coerced.

## 9. Guardianship Rules

- Never say "AI-powered." Say "on-device intelligence" or "your personal engine."
- Never show a cloud icon. Ever. Not even in diagrams.
- No gradients that look like generic tech startups. Only the Enrichment Violet, used sparingly and specifically for transformation states.
- No stock photography of people with laptops.
- Always demonstrate privacy through architecture; never just claim it.
- Pricing must be whole-dollar. Payment systems must match.
- The Creator fee is never a "tax" or "cut." It is a "contribution."
- The Provenance Seal (not "verified badge") is algorithmic and transparent — a stamp of duration and subscriber trust, not human gatekeeping.
