# Monetization.md — Gearbox Relay

**Status:** Canonical (May 2026)  
**Owner:** Gearbox.Ai  
**Version:** 1.1  

---

## 1. Philosophy

Gearbox Relay is built on the belief that a tool for the mind should be **fair, transparent, and never extractive**. We do not rent out your attention, sell your data, or hide costs behind manipulative pricing. Every dollar we earn must feel like a contribution to a well‑maintained transmission—the honest fuel that keeps the relay running.

Our pricing follows three principles:

- **Functional generosity** – the free tier is a complete, private knowledge tool; paying is for expanding your reach, not unlocking basic utility.
- **Whole‑dollar integrity** – no .99 trickery. Prices are round numbers, like a machinist's invoice.
- **Radical transparency** – where possible, costs are disclosed. The Creator fee is a voluntary sliding scale anchored to real infrastructure costs, not an opaque platform tax.

## 2. Pricing Tiers (Launch)

### 2.1 Free Tier

*For everyone who wants to capture, enrich, and curate privately.*

- Unlimited highlights, screenshots, and copied text
- On‑device AI: tagging, summarization, connection suggestions
- Full‑text and semantic search
- **Up to 3 published Streams**  
- 1 device, 50 MB sync storage (enough for thousands of text highlights)
- Community support

**This tier never expires.** We will never pressure users with time‑limited trials. The free tier is a fully functional transmission for a single operator.

### 2.2 Pro Tier (Founding Curator – Launch Phase)

*For the earliest users who help us build the network.*

- **Everything in Free, plus:**
- Unlimited Streams (publish as many curated transmissions as you like)
- Advanced AI enrichment (custom taxonomies, deeper connection mapping)
- Themed Review sessions (AI‑curated spaced repetition)
- Multi‑device sync (desktop + mobile with 5 GB encrypted storage)
- Analytics for your published Streams
- Data export (Markdown, JSON, ZIP)
- Priority support and early access to new features

**Founding Curator Price:**  
$60 / year (equivalent to $5/month), available during the first 3 months after public launch **or until the first 5,000 subscribers, whichever comes first.**  
This price is **grandfathered for life** as long as the subscription remains active. Founding Curators who cancel and later re‑subscribe will return at the standard rate.

**Standard Pro Price (after launch window):**  
$8 / month or **$80 / year** ($96 equivalent — 16.7% discount). A 7‑day free trial is available for Themed Review and multi‑device sync triggers.

### 2.3 Creator Tier (Add‑on to Pro)

*For curators who want to turn their Streams into a sustainable signal.*

- All Pro features
- **Monetizable Streams** – set a monthly subscription price for each paid Stream
- Audience analytics dashboard
- **Provenance Seal** – an algorithmic, time‑based trust indicator (not a human "verified badge")
- Direct relationship with subscribers (email notifications, community features)

**Creator Platform Fee (Beta):**  
0% during the beta period, which lasts for **12 months from public launch**. After 12 months, the sliding scale activates regardless of total creator earnings. We will not take a single cent from your subscriber revenue during your first year building the network — this is concrete proof that we're not an extractive platform before the value is proven.

**Post‑Beta Fee: The Transmission Fuel Model**  
After the beta period, a sliding‑scale fee activates. Creators choose their contribution rate:

| Level | Rate | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Minimum** | 10% | Covers direct processing costs (Stripe fees, bandwidth, storage) |
| **Suggested** | 15% | Fuels ongoing development, open‑source maintenance, and community support |
| **Generous** | 20%+ | Actively invests in the future of the platform and is recognized in a public "Builders" list (if desired) |

Creators can change their rate at any time. The average contribution will be published on a live public dashboard, but individual choices remain private. No algorithmic coercion, no hidden quotas. This is a voluntary transmission sustainer, not a tax.

## 3. Paywall Triggers

Paywalls appear only when a user attempts to exceed the generous boundaries of the free tier. Each trigger is non‑intrusive and explains the value of Pro.

| Trigger | Message | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Publishing 4th Stream | "You've built three transmissions. To create unlimited Streams and unlock advanced curation tools, upgrade to Pro." | Offer Founding Curator (during launch) or standard Pro, with 7‑day trial |
| Adding 2nd device for sync | "Multi‑device sync keeps your context moving across all your machines. Unlock 5 GB of encrypted sync with Pro." | 7‑day free trial of Pro |
| Starting first Themed Review | "Themed Review is a Pro feature. Try it free for 7 days, then continue with Pro." | 7‑day free trial |
| Requesting data export (Markdown/JSON) | "Export your knowledge graph. Pro includes full data portability." | Offer Pro |
| Attempting to monetize a Stream | "Monetizing Streams requires a Pro membership and Creator features. Join Pro and start building your subscriber base." | Offer Pro (Creator features included) |

All triggers are **dismissible**; users can continue using Free indefinitely. Triggers do not interrupt the core capture loop.

## 4. Creator Monetization Mechanics

### 4.1 Setting Up a Paid Stream
- A Pro member can mark any Stream as "Paid" and set a monthly subscription price (minimum $3, maximum $50).
- The Creator keeps 100% during the beta fee holiday. Post‑beta, the Creator's take‑home is: `subscriber_price * (1 − chosen_fee_rate)`.

### 4.2 Subscriber Experience
- Subscribers can pay via credit card or crypto (USDC). The subscription is managed through a standard billing system; Gearbox Relay never sees the subscriber's plaintext highlights or private data.
- Subscribers get access to the paid Stream content (articles, highlights, commentary) that is curated and enriched by the creator.

### 4.3 Transparency Dashboard (Post‑Beta)
- Creators see a detailed monthly statement: "Your Stream had 42 subscribers at $5. Your 15% contribution ($31.50) helped cover: sync ($8.40), storage ($3.15), bandwidth ($4.20), open‑source maintenance ($10.50), feature development ($5.25)."
- A public, anonymized "Transmission Fuel" page shows aggregate platform costs and average contribution rates.

## 5. Early Adopter Programs

### 5.1 Founding Curator
- Available for the first 3 months after public launch.
- $60/year, grandfathered for life.
- Founding Curators receive a special profile marker (a subtle gear icon) and are acknowledged as the community's original builders. They are also invited to a private feedback channel with the development team.

### 5.2 Creator Beta Fee Holiday
- 0% platform fee until the earlier of 12 months post‑launch or $1M cumulative creator earnings.
- Early creators build their revenue base without any cut, encouraging experimentation and high‑quality Streams during the critical liquidity‑building phase.

### 5.3 Referral Program (Future)
- A privacy‑preserving referral system (e.g., sharing a Stream that naturally invites subscriptions) will be considered post‑launch. No financial incentives will be tied to user data or surveillance.

## 6. Pricing Psychology & Brand Alignment

- **Whole‑dollar prices** avoid the deceptive feel of .99 endings. They reflect the honest, mechanical character of the brand.
- **No per‑seat, enterprise‑grade tiers.** Gearbox Relay is for individuals and their trusted networks. The pricing is simple and personal, not a corporate menu.
- **The voluntary sliding fee** for Creators is a direct expression of our Outlaw/Artisan ethos—we trust you to support the infrastructure you use, and we show you exactly where your support goes.
- **Free tier generosity** is a strategic moat: users who have built a rich knowledge graph and published even a few Streams are deeply invested. Upgrading feels natural, not coerced.

## 7. Future Considerations

- **Enterprise / team plans** – Not currently planned. If demanded, any team plan would maintain on‑device AI and local‑first principles; data would never be centrally pooled.
- **API access for custom model integration** – Potential Pro+ feature, enabling technically adept users to swap in their own fine‑tuned local models. Would be priced as an add‑on with transparent computation costs.
- **Marketplace for premium Streams** – The Creator tier already supports individual paid Streams. A centralized discovery marketplace may be introduced, with the same sliding‑scale fee structure and enhanced curation tools. No additional hidden fees.
